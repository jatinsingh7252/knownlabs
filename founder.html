The Founder

Jatin Singh

Founder, KnownLabs Bengaluru, India Designer × Technologist

I'm Jatin — a designer who codes and a technologist who obsesses over pixels. I started KnownLabs because I kept watching good businesses lose to bad websites, disconnected freelancers, and marketing that looked nice but converted nothing. So I built the agency I wished existed: one team where design and technology sit in the same room.

Creative · Technical · Entrepreneurial

"Design decides how it feels. Technology decides how far it goes. I build where the two meet."

— Jatin Singh
