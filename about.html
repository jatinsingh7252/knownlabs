Our story

One partner instead of five vendors.

Why KnownLabs exists — in three short paragraphs.

KnownLabs started with a frustration every business owner knows: you hire a designer, then a developer, then someone for SEO — and spend your weeks project-managing people who've never met each other.

We built the agency we wished existed. One team that owns your brand end to end — the identity, the website, the content, and the growth strategy — led directly by our founder on every project.

Based in Bengaluru and working with clients across India, we blend creative craft with technical rigour: design that looks sharp, engineering that's solid underneath, and every decision tied to getting your brand known.

What we believe

Four rules we never break.

01

Craft over shortcuts

No bloated templates, no copy-paste work. Everything we ship is designed and built with care — because your brand deserves better than "good enough".

02

Honesty, always

Clear quotes, realistic timelines, straight answers. If something won't work for your business, we'll tell you before you pay for it.

03

Built to rank

Pretty is table stakes. Every website leaves our hands fast, mobile-first, and structured so Google can actually find it.

04

Relationships, not transactions

Launch day is the start, not the end. We support, maintain, and grow with the brands we build — for the long run.
